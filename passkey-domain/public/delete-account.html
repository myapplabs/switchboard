Switchboard Terms · Privacy

Delete your Switchboard account

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Switchboard accounts live on a hub, the server that you, or the person who invited you, run. AppLabs, LLC doesn't hold your account, so deletion happens on your hub.

In the app

  1. Open Switchboard and go to More, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account, and confirm.

What is deleted

  • The conversations you own, on the hub and on the agents, and the files and photos sent in them.
  • Your access to agents, your quick commands, and your sign-in on every device: your passkey registration, devices and notification tokens on the hub.
  • Your name, which is replaced with "Deleted member" wherever it still appears, such as the activity log (kept 90 days) and board cards you created. The cards stay, for the people you worked with.

If you own the hub, your account can't exist without it, so deleting it deletes everything on the hub: every member's account and conversations, the agents' settings and keys, jobs, cards and settings. You confirm by typing the hub's name.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, ask the person who runs your hub to remove you (More, Members, your name, Remove member). If you run the hub, you can delete everything by uninstalling the hub and deleting its data folder.

Information AppLabs holds

AppLabs doesn't store your account or conversations. The notification relay doesn't store notifications or tokens, and deleting your account removes your notification token from the hub, so no more notifications are sent. To ask about or delete anything else we hold, such as emails you sent us, write to mail@applabs.llc.

Your passkey stays in your device's password manager (for example, Google Password Manager or iCloud Keychain) until you delete it there.
