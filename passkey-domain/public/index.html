What it does

A control room for the agents you already run

Hermes Agent first, with more agent types to come. Switchboard doesn't replace your agents: it connects your people to them, safely.

01

Chat from anywhere

Streaming answers, tool activity as it happens, model switching, and conversations kept on your own hub.

02

Share without sharing keys

Invite members with a QR code. Roles and per-agent grants decide who uses which agent and model. Agent keys never leave the hub.

03

Approvals reach the right person

When an agent wants to run something risky, the request goes to someone allowed to decide, on their phone.

04

Agents that work together

Give a card to one agent or several in turn, so the research model hands off to the coding model. A coordinator can plan the work; you approve the plan.

05

You decide when it's done

Agents never mark their own work finished. Every card ends in your review: accept, send back with a note, or close.

06

Jobs, quick commands, Ask all

Manage scheduled jobs, keep prompt templates, and ask every agent the same question side by side.

How it works

Three pieces, all yours but the app

The hub is a small program on a computer you own. Your phone talks only to the hub, and the hub talks to your agents.

The appPhones

Sign in with a passkey: no passwords. Everyone sees only the agents they're allowed to use.

Your hubYour computer

Holds the agent keys, the conversations and the rules. Reached privately over Tailscale.

Your agentsYour machines

Hermes Agent today, on any models you like. Each agent can run a different one.

The board

Work moves across the board, and you stay in the loop

Each step is an ordinary conversation you can open and steer. Limits on every card keep agents from looping or overspending.

Privacy

Your conversations stay on your hub

Switchboard is built so the people who make it never see what you and your agents say.

  • Conversations, agent keys and accounts live on the hub you run, not on AppLabs' servers.
  • Notifications carry no content, only "an approval is waiting". The app fetches the details from your hub.
  • Passkeys instead of passwords. This domain only vouches for the official app; it holds no accounts or data.
  • Members' chats are private by default, with separate agent memory for each person.
