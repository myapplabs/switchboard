## 2. What Switchboard is

Switchboard lets you use AI agents that you, or someone you trust, run on your own computers. It has three parts:

- **The hub**: open-source server software that you, or the person who invited you (the "hub owner"), install and run. The hub keeps accounts, permissions and conversations, and talks to the agents.
- **The App**: connects to a hub. It doesn't work without one.
- **The agents**: third-party software, such as Hermes Agent, that the hub owner connects. Agents use AI models from providers the hub owner chooses.

AppLabs doesn't host, run or have access to your hub, your agents or your conversations. There is no Switchboard account with AppLabs. Your account lives on a hub, and the hub owner controls it.
