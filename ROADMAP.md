# Roadmap

Where Switchboard is going. Plans change; issues and discussions are welcome.

## Done

- **Hub and chat:** pairing, passkeys, agents with health and capabilities, streaming chat with tool
  activity, stop, steer and questions, conversation history.
- **Members:** invites, roles, per-agent grants (profile, models, daily limits), private and shared
  conversations, an activity log.
- **Control:** approvals routed to the right person, the model picker, jobs, quick commands.
- **Notifications:** content-free push through a relay; Ask all; accessibility.
- **The board:** cards with steps on different agents, review before done, limits on every card, a
  coordinator agent that plans sub-cards, and FORGE tickets as cards.

## Next

- **Releases:** Android on Google Play (testing first, then public), iOS on the App Store.
- **Easier hosting:** a one-line hub installer, guided pairing, and an option to reach a hub without
  Tailscale.
- **More agents:** OpenClaw and OpenAI-compatible agents through new adapters.
- **More from the phone:** the share sheet, voice, widgets, several hubs in one app.

## Later

- An adapter SDK so the community can add agent types.
- Features for organisations: single sign-on, custom roles, audit export.
